    At closing time

    Picture a tandoor in G-9 with 40 naan left at 10 pm, or a bakery in F-7 Markaz with trays of bread and mithai that won't sell tomorrow. The food is good, but the day is over.

    Some of it goes to staff or is sold off cheaply. What's left can end up in the bin, because there's no quick, trusted way to get it to someone who needs it before its time runs out.

  • {% include icon.html name="users" size=20 %}

    Across the city

    NGOs, community kitchens, shelters and iftar dastarkhwans feed people every day. Good food at half of retail or less would stretch what they can do.

    But surplus appears late and lasts only hours, and there's no easy way to find it, check that it's safe, or be sure it will still be there when they arrive.

The gap isn't the food. It's time, trust and coordination. And there's no Good Samaritan law in Pakistan to protect a business that hands food on, so whoever passes it on carries the risk.

{% include section-head.html eyebrow="What we'll do about it" title="What Rizq Rasan will do" id="answer-title" lead="Rizq Rasan will connect verified businesses that have surplus food with verified NGOs nearby, starting in Islamabad on 22 March 2027." %}
  • Businesses list what's left, with a photo, a pickup window and a safe-until time.
  • NGOs nearby claim it, and pay no more than half of what it sells for in the shop.
  • Every business and every NGO is checked before they can list or claim.
  • Each kind of food has a time limit, and cooked food opens only after the pilot's first 4 weeks pass with no food incident.
  • Food is handed over only for the right 4-digit pickup code.
  • Vendors recover part of their cost, which gives them a reason to list again tomorrow.
  • Every completed pickup adds to public totals, with the method on our Impact page.

{% include section-head.html title="How we check these figures" id="checking-title" %}

Each figure on this page is footnoted with its source and year. We recheck every one on its source page before launch, and update them when newer figures come out.

Where sources disagree, we leave the figure out. That's why you won't find a food-waste-per-person figure here: published estimates for Pakistan differ too widely. If you spot a figure that's out of date, tell us and we'll check it.
