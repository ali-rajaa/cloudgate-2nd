This policy explains how Rizq Rasan checks the businesses and organisations that use the platform: who has to be verified, which documents we ask for, the checks we run, how long it takes, and what happens afterwards. It forms part of our [Terms of Service]({{ '/legal/terms/' | relative_url }}).

Accounts are open now. Create one and upload your documents through it, and verification starts. Listing and claiming food start at launch on 22 March 2027, or earlier for organisations invited to the January 2027 pilot. Please don't attach documents to the forms on this website or email them to us.

## 1. Why we verify

Rizq Rasan sells food rather than giving it away, so Pakistan's food sale laws apply in full. Every business that lists food must be properly registered and licensed, and every organisation that claims food must be a genuine non-profit that serves or gives the food to people for free.

Verification also protects everyone against fraud, resale of food and misuse of charities. Charities are a focus of Pakistan's anti-terror-financing rules. FBR's 2025 rules for online marketplaces may require an NTN before we can pay a business through the platform; we will confirm this with our lawyer before payments move onto the platform, and ask for an NTN then if it is needed.

Verification lowers the risk of dealing with a bad actor, but it cannot guarantee how any organisation will behave. Our [Disclaimer]({{ '/legal/disclaimer/' | relative_url }}) explains this.

## 2. Who must be verified

- **Every vendor and every NGO.** No organisation can list food, see listings or claim food until we have approved it.
- **The organisation, not the person.** The organisation is the account holder. It is what we verify, and it is responsible for everything done through its account.
- **The person who signs.** Everyone who signs for an organisation must be at least 18 years old and allowed to act for it. We check their CNIC. If they aren't the owner or the head of the organisation, we also ask for an authority letter.
- **Staff, collectors and payers.** A vendor's counter staff, an NGO's 1 to 5 collectors and its up to 3 named payers are not verified one by one. The organisation that adds them is responsible for them.

Who can join:

- **Vendors:** restaurants, bakeries, grocers and supermarkets, and cafes in Islamabad. Hotels and caterers will be able to join once our safety processes are proven. Home kitchens cannot join, because home-cooked food is never allowed. At launch each vendor lists from one pickup point.
- **NGOs:** registered NGOs, shelters, community kitchens, food banks and orphanages in Islamabad that serve or give food to people for free and promise never to resell it.

Neither the organisation nor anyone who signs for it may appear on the UN Security Council sanctions list or Pakistan's proscribed-organisations or Fourth Schedule lists.

## 3. Documents for vendors

| Document | Who needs it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner's CNIC | Every vendor | Front and back in one photo or file |
| One proof of the business | Every vendor | Any one of: Islamabad Food Authority licence, NTN certificate, SECP certificate or partnership deed, or a recent utility bill for the shop |
| Where NGOs pay you | Every vendor | A Raast ID or an IBAN, entered in the form |
| Islamabad Food Authority licence | Optional | Recommended. It helps us approve you faster |
| Halal certificate | Optional | Your listings then show "halal-certified" instead of "vendor-declared" |

## 4. Documents for NGOs

### Registration we accept

You must be registered under one of these routes. Registration under the ICT Charities Act 2021 is preferred for organisations based in Islamabad.

| Registration route | Registered with | How we check it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICT Charities Registration, Regulation and Facilitation Act 2021 | The ICT charities registration authority | We check the certificate and call the registrar, as there is no online register |
| Societies Registration Act 1860 | The Registrar, ICT Administration | We check the certificate and call the registrar |
| ICT Trust Act 2020 | The Registrar of Trusts, ICT Administration | We check the certificate and call the registrar |
| Section 42 of the Companies Act 2017 | SECP, as a licensed not-for-profit company | SECP's online company search |
| Voluntary Social Welfare Agencies Ordinance 1961 | The social welfare authority | We check the certificate and call the registrar |
| International NGO | Ministry of Interior | We check your registration letter and your MoU with the Ministry |

International NGOs register with the Ministry of Interior, not the Economic Affairs Division. If that is you, send your Ministry of Interior registration letter and MoU as your registration certificate.

### What to send

1. **Registration certificate** under one of the routes above.
2. **The CNIC of the person in charge.**
3. **An authority letter,** only if someone other than the head of the organisation will use Rizq Rasan.
4. **For Tier A only:** your Pakistan Centre for Philanthropy certificate or FBR approval under section 2(36).

If you cook or reheat food, you may also need an Islamabad Food Authority licence. We will confirm this with a lawyer before launch and update this policy.

## 5. Sending your documents

- **One upload for each document,** by camera or from a file, in your account's onboarding task list. You can save and come back at any time.
- **File types:** JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB each. A file over 10 MB will not upload.
- **Readable and complete:** send every page, uncropped, with names, numbers and dates easy to read. The names and numbers must match what you entered in the form.
- **Genuine documents only:** never upload a document that is forged, altered or belongs to someone else. Uploading forged documents means permanent removal from Rizq Rasan.

## 6. The checks we run

Our verification team reviews every application by hand, with a checklist for each document.

- **Matching:** we compare names, numbers and dates across your documents and your form.
- **Registers:** we search SECP's online register for companies, including Section 42 companies. Where no online register exists, we call the registrar.
- **Tax:** if you send an NTN, we check it on FBR's Active Taxpayer List.
- **Licences:** if you send an Islamabad Food Authority licence, we keep a copy on file and may spot-check it with the Authority.
- **Sanctions screening:** we screen every NGO, and every signatory of a vendor or NGO, against the UN Security Council sanctions list and Pakistan's proscribed-organisations and Fourth Schedule lists.
- **A call or visit:** for each of our first 20 partners, we also hold a video call or visit your premises, kitchen or distribution site.

## 7. How long it takes

We aim to decide within **2 working days** of receiving a complete application, with every required document. Working days follow our office hours: {{ site.contact.hours }}. While a vendor waits, it can prepare draft listings.

We email you at each step. If something is wrong, we list each problem word for word, and only that section of your application reopens. You fix it and resubmit, and we review the change.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Email not verified | You have signed up but not yet confirmed your email address |
| Onboarding | You are filling in your details and uploading documents |
| Pending review | You have submitted, and our team is checking your application |
| Needs changes | We found a problem. The email lists each one, and only those sections reopen |
| Approved | You can start listing or claiming food. NGOs start in Tier C |
| Rejected | We cannot approve the application. We tell you why and how to appeal |
| Suspended | At any time after approval: your account is paused, with a reason and an end date |
| Removed | At any time after approval: your account is closed permanently |

## 8. Trust tiers for NGOs

Every NGO is placed in a trust tier, which sets how much food it can claim. Every organisation starts in Tier C, including one that already qualifies for Tier A.

| Tier | Who it's for | Unpaid claims at one time | Claims a day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier C | New organisations, for the first month or the first 10 completed pickups, whichever is later | 1 | 2 |
| Tier B | Registered and verified organisations, after Tier C | 2 | 6 |
| Tier A | Organisations certified by the Pakistan Centre for Philanthropy, or approved by FBR under section 2(36) | 3 | No limit |

How to move up:

- **Tier C to Tier B:** once you have been approved for a month and have completed 10 pickups, you move to Tier B.
- **To Tier A:** after Tier C, upload your Pakistan Centre for Philanthropy certificate or FBR section 2(36) approval. Once we have checked it, you move to Tier A.
- **We email you** whenever your tier changes.

If an NGO's reliability score falls below 60, it drops to Tier C limits until our team reviews its account. Our [Reliability and No-show Policy]({{ '/legal/reliability/' | relative_url }}) explains the score. Vendors have no tiers, but a vendor whose score falls below 60 can have at most 3 live listings until we review it.

Later, Rizq Rasan may offer pay-later invoices to Tier A NGOs with a strong reliability score. This is not part of the platform at launch, when every NGO pays at claim.

## 9. Staying verified

- **Tell us about changes.** Keep your details and documents up to date, and tell us when your signatory, address, registration or bank account changes. Upload the new document in your account.
- **Payout accounts.** A vendor's payout account must always be in the business name. When it changes, payouts pause for 48 hours and the owner gets an email, to stop fraud.
- **Expiry reminders.** If a document you sent has an expiry date, our team notes it and reminds you before it runs out, so you have time to upload a new one.
- **Expired documents.** Keep the documents you sent up to date. If a required document expires and you have not uploaded a new one, you no longer meet our eligibility rules, and we can suspend your account until you do.
- **Every 12 months.** We re-verify every organisation once a year. We ask you to confirm your details, replace any out-of-date documents, and we run our checks again, including sanctions screening.

## 10. Who can see your documents

- **Only our verification team.** Your documents are stored privately and never shown to other vendors, NGOs or the public. Our team opens them only through links that expire after 5 minutes. From launch, every view will be logged.
- **CNICs are protected.** CNIC numbers are encrypted and shown masked, for example 61101-XXXXXXX-1.
- **What you see.** After you upload a document, your account shows its review status, and you can replace it.
- **What other organisations see.** Other organisations see that you are verified, and your reliability badge. They never see your documents. An NGO's address is never shown to vendors.
- **Sharing outside Rizq Rasan.** We share documents only as our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/legal/privacy/' | relative_url }}) explains, for example when the law requires it.
- **How long we keep them.** We keep documents for the life of your account plus 6 years, as section 174 of the Income Tax Ordinance requires. Our [Data Retention]({{ '/legal/data-retention/' | relative_url }}) policy has the details.

## 11. Rejection, suspension and removal

We may reject an application when, for example:

- the organisation is not a food business or a registered non-profit operating in Islamabad;
- a required document, such as the owner's CNIC or proof that the business is real, is missing;
- the documents do not match each other or cannot be checked;
- the organisation or a signatory appears on a sanctions or proscribed list;
- an NGO would sell food on, rather than serve or give it for free.

After approval, we can suspend or remove an organisation that no longer meets these rules or breaks our [Terms of Service]({{ '/legal/terms/' | relative_url }}). A suspension comes with a written reason and an end date, and a suspended organisation keeps read access to its records. If a vendor is suspended, its listings are hidden and its paid orders are refunded. If an NGO is suspended, its paid orders can still be collected unless we decide otherwise.

Uploading forged documents or reselling claimed food means permanent removal.

## 12. Appeals

- **Rejection.** The rejection email tells you why and how to appeal. Reply to it, or write to {{ site.contact.email }}, and explain why you think we got it wrong, with any new documents. We handle an appeal as a complaint, so we acknowledge it within 1 working day and aim to resolve it within 5 working days.
- **Suspension.** You can appeal a suspension within 7 days, as our [Reliability and No-show Policy]({{ '/legal/reliability/' | relative_url }}) explains.
- **A person decides.** A member of our team reviews every appeal and sends you the decision and the reason in writing.
- **Applying again.** If you were rejected because something was missing, such as a licence, you can apply again once you have it.

If you are still not satisfied, our [Complaints and Disputes]({{ '/legal/complaints/' | relative_url }}) policy explains how to take it further.
