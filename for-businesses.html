{% include section-head.html title="Food businesses in Islamabad, big or small" id="who-title" %}

Restaurants, bakeries, cafes, grocers and supermarkets can list. Hotels and caterers will join once our safety processes are proven. You need your CNIC and one proof that your business is real. A Food Authority licence or an NTN helps, but you can join without them.

We're starting close to the first NGOs we sign, in two zones: F-6, F-7 and Blue Area, and G-9 to G-11, F-10 and F-11. Bakeries come first, because their surplus is daily. Accounts are open now to businesses anywhere in Islamabad, and listing starts at launch.

At launch, each business lists from one pickup point. If you run several branches, tell us when you create your account.

{% include section-head.html title="What you'll need to send" id="documents-title" lead="Three things, all from your phone. Verification takes 2 working days. If something is missing, we'll tell you exactly what, and only that part needs redoing." %}
  • Your CNIC: the owner's, front and back in one photo.
  • One proof of your business: any one of a Food Authority licence, an NTN certificate, a business registration, or a recent utility bill for the shop.
  • Where NGOs pay you: a Raast ID or a bank account (IBAN).
  • Optional: your Islamabad Food Authority licence if you have one, which helps us approve you faster, and a halal certificate, so your listings show "halal-certified" instead of "vendor-declared".

After you create your account, you upload each document in the app by camera or file, as a JPG, PNG or PDF of up to 10 MB. Only our verification team sees them, and they're stored privately.

We'll also do a video call or a site visit with each of our first 20 partners.

{% include section-head.html title="Food rules in brief" id="food-title" lead="Selling food, rather than giving it away, means food laws apply in full. So the rules are strict from day one." %}
  • {% include icon.html name="check" size=20 %}

    What you can list from the start of the pilot

    Tandoor breads, plain bakery, mithai, fruit and vegetables, packaged goods and drinks. Cooked food such as rice, curries and fried snacks is planned from 1 February 2027, if the pilot's first 4 weeks pass with no food incident.

  • {% include icon.html name="x" size=20 %}

    Never allowed

    Home-cooked food, plate returns or part-eaten food, food past its use-by date, opened dairy drinks, recalled products, alcohol and tobacco. No raw meat, poultry or fish at launch.

  • {% include icon.html name="thermometer" size=20 %}

    A time limit for every category

    Safe-until fills in from the category limit. You can shorten it but never extend it, and pickup must end at least 30 minutes before it. Cooked food can spend at most 2 hours at room temperature, and from May to September it must be hot-held or chilled.

  • {% include icon.html name="shield" size=20 %}

    Yours until handover

    Before publishing, you confirm the food was never served to customers, isn't a plate return, and has been stored correctly. You tell us about any food incident or Islamabad Food Authority action within 24 hours.
