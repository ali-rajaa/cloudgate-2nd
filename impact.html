{% include section-head.html title="How each number is worked out" id="method-title" lead="Every number starts from the weight of food collected, and the others are worked out from it with published factors. Totals come straight from completed orders, so nobody types them in by hand." %}
  • {% include icon.html name="leaf" size=20 %}

    Kilograms of food rescued

    Every listing carries the vendor's estimate of its weight, with a helper that suggests a figure: 40 naan is about 6 kg. A weight more than 3 times the helper's estimate sends the listing to our team for review.

    A listing counts only once its pickup code has been entered at handover and the order is completed. Food that expires, is cancelled or isn't collected adds nothing.

  • {% include icon.html name="heart" size=20 %}

    Meal equivalents

    One meal for every 420 g of food. This follows WRAP's meal-equivalent guidance, the most cautious published factor.

    Where a vendor lists portions, we count the portions, but never more than the weight divided by 0.42 kg, so small portions can't inflate the count. A meal equivalent measures an amount of food. It isn't a claim about a balanced meal.

  • {% include icon.html name="globe" size=20 %}

    CO2e avoided

    2.5 kg of CO2e for every kg of food. It comes from FAO's Food Wastage Footprint, which puts the footprint of 1.3 billion tonnes of wasted food at 3.3 billion tonnes of CO2e.

    It's an average across all kinds of food, so treat it as an estimate rather than a measurement. CO2e means greenhouse gas counted as the amount of carbon dioxide with the same warming effect.

  • {% include icon.html name="receipt" size=20 %}

    Rupees saved by NGOs

    The food's retail value minus the price the NGO paid. Vendors state the retail value on every listing, and the price can be at most half of it, so an NGO always saves at least half of what the food would cost in the shop.

  • {% include icon.html name="users" size=20 %}

    Active partners

    The verified vendors and NGOs taking part through completed orders. We'll state the exact period it covers alongside the first published totals.

  • {% include icon.html name="shield" size=20 %}

    Who publishes them

    The public numbers change only when someone on our team checks and publishes them, once a week. Weight is always shown next to meals, so you can see what each figure rests on.

{% include icon.html name="alert" size=20 %}

These are estimates. Weights are entered by vendors, and the meal and carbon factors are published averages, not measurements of each pickup. Read our disclaimer for what the numbers can and can't tell you.
