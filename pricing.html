5%

Rizq Rasan keeps 5% of the listing price. The vendor keeps 95%, before any tax withheld by law.

The 5% includes services tax and is rounded down to the whole rupee, in the vendor's favour. Out of it we pay the payment gateway's fees on normal sales.

{% include section-head.html title="Worked example: a Rs 10,000 claim" id="example-title" lead="An NGO claims a listing priced at Rs 10,000. Because the price can be at most half of retail, the same food would sell for at least Rs 20,000 in the shop." %}

The NGO pays Rs 10,000 when it claims. After pickup, the vendor receives Rs 9,500 and Rizq Rasan keeps Rs 500. The gateway's fee comes out of our Rs 500, not out of the vendor's share.

The vendor's Rs 9,500 is before any tax the law requires us to withhold. If tax is withheld, it's shown line by line on the vendor's statement.

Our share is always rounded down. On a Rs 1,250 listing, for example: NGO pays Rs 1,250 · Vendor receives Rs 1,188 · Rizq Rasan Rs 62.

{% include section-head.html title="Fees" id="fees-title" %}
  • {% include icon.html name="receipt" size=20 %}

    No sign-up or monthly fees

    Joining is free for businesses and NGOs. Rizq Rasan earns its 5% only when a listing is sold.

  • {% include icon.html name="scale" size=20 %}

    We pay the gateway fees

    The payment gateway charges a fee on every payment. On normal sales Rizq Rasan pays it out of the 5%, so it never comes out of the vendor's share or adds to the NGO's price.

    Gateways keep their fee when a payment is refunded, so whoever caused a refund bears that fee. The refund table below shows who that is.

  • {% include icon.html name="doc" size=20 %}

    Tax shown line by line

    If the law requires us to withhold tax from a vendor's share, we deduct it and show it on the vendor's statement. Every payout comes with a tax invoice for our commission.

During the pilot in January 2027, NGOs will pay vendors directly and Rizq Rasan will charge no commission. The 5% starts once payments move onto the platform.

Raast Default

Approve a payment request in your own banking app, or scan a QR code. The money arrives in seconds, well inside the payment deadline.

{% include icon.html name="phone" size=20 %}

JazzCash or Easypaisa

Pay from a JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet, through PayFast. This is the second choice at checkout.

{% include icon.html name="clock" size=20 %}

Cards later

Cards come in Phase 2, after launch. They cost about three times as much as Raast, bring chargebacks that can arrive up to 240 days later, and most NGOs pay from bank accounts anyway.

{% include icon.html name="x" size=20 %}

No cash at pickup

Paying when you claim is what holds the food for you and protects vendors from no-shows. We can't confirm a cash payment or give a proper receipt for it, so cash is never taken at the counter.

Over-the-counter vouchers and Easypaisa shop payments aren't offered, because they take days to clear and food can't wait that long.

{% include section-head.html eyebrow="Listing prices" title="Minimum and maximum price" id="limits-title" %}

Every listing is priced between Rs 1,000 and half of what the food would sell for in the shop. The vendor gets part of its cost back, and the NGO never pays more than half of retail.

Small orders take the same checking and support time as big ones, which is why listings start at Rs 1,000. We'll recheck the minimum once the payment gateway confirms its fees in writing.

Each listing is one batch of food, sold whole to one NGO. Splitting a listing between NGOs comes later, in Phase 2.

  2. Order completed

    The NGO confirms it received everything, or 24 hours pass with no problem reported.

    Within 24 hours

  3. Money settles

    The payment gateway settles the money into Rizq Rasan's collections account, which is kept apart from company money.

    Usually 2 to 3 working days after payment

  4. Payout

    Your share goes out in the next Monday or Thursday run, with a statement listing every order.

    Within 6 working days of pickup

How payouts are kept safe

  • Payouts go only to a bank account in your business's name.
  • One person on our team prepares each payout run and a second approves it, so no one can move money alone.
  • If an order is under dispute, only that order waits. Your other orders are paid as normal.
  • Each statement lists every order, our commission, any tax withheld and the bank reference.
  • If an NGO doesn't turn up, you're still paid for the food once the no-show is confirmed.

If you change your payout bank account, payouts pause for 48 hours, and we email the business owner to let them know.
