## 3. What Rizq Rasan does

Rizq Rasan is a technology platform that connects food businesses that have surplus food with verified NGOs that feed people.

- **We are an intermediary.** The sale of food is between the vendor and the NGO. We do not prepare, cook, own, store, pack, transport or inspect food, and we never become the owner of it.
- **We verify, set rules and settle disputes.** We check organisations before they can use the platform, set the listing and safety rules, and decide disputes fairly under our published policies.
- **We collect payment for the vendor.** The vendor appoints Rizq Rasan as its limited agent to collect payment from the NGO. When an NGO pays Rizq Rasan for an order, that counts as paying the vendor. The details are in the [Vendor Agreement]({{ '/legal/vendor-agreement/' | relative_url }}).
- **We hold no balances.** No user keeps a balance or wallet with us. Money collected for vendors sits in a collections account kept apart from company money until it is paid out.

## 4. Who can use the platform

Rizq Rasan is for organisations, not for individuals buying food for themselves. To use it:

- you must be a food business or a non-profit organisation registered in Pakistan and operating in Islamabad, and you must pass our [verification]({{ '/legal/verification/' | relative_url }});
- vendors must hold an NTN and an Islamabad Food Authority licence, and restaurants and cafes also their Department of Tourist Services registration;
- NGOs must be registered under one of the routes in the [NGO Agreement]({{ '/legal/ngo-agreement/' | relative_url }});
- every person who opens an account or signs for an organisation must be at least 18 years old and allowed to act for it, shown by a board resolution or authority letter;
- neither the organisation nor its signatories may appear on the UN Security Council sanctions list or Pakistan's proscribed-organisations or Fourth Schedule lists. We screen every NGO and every signatory against these lists.

We can refuse an application, and we explain why when we do.

## 6. Listings and prices

Only approved vendors can list food, and only approved NGOs can see listings. Live listings never appear on this public website. Every listing must follow these rules:

- **Price.** At least Rs 1,000 and no more than 50% of the food's retail value, in whole rupees. The price is fixed once the food is claimed.
- **Sold whole.** Each listing is one batch of food at one pickup point, sold whole to one NGO.
- **True description.** A real photo of the actual food (1 to 3 photos, the first required), the right category, quantity and weight, allergens, meat content and halal status. Halal shows as "vendor-declared" unless the vendor has uploaded a halal certificate.
- **Safe-until.** Set from the category's time limit. A vendor can shorten it but never extend it.
- **Pickup window.** At least 30 minutes long, ending at least 60 minutes after publishing and at least 30 minutes before the safe-until time.
- **Allowed food only.** No raw meat, poultry or fish at launch, and never home-cooked food, plate returns or food past its use-by date. The full list is in the [Food Safety Policy]({{ '/legal/food-safety-policy/' | relative_url }}).

We may hold a listing for review before it goes live, correct an obvious error, or remove a listing that breaks the rules. We tell the vendor the reason.

## 7. Claims, the payment deadline and when a sale is made

- **A claim is a short reservation, not a sale.** When an NGO claims a listing, we hold the food for that NGO until the payment deadline: 30 minutes after claiming, or 15 minutes before pickup ends, whichever comes first.
- **No claim without time to pay.** If less than 10 minutes would be left to pay, the listing cannot be claimed.
- **The sale is made at payment.** The contract of sale between the vendor and the NGO is formed only when the payment is confirmed by our payment gateway.
- **If the deadline passes unpaid,** the reservation ends, the order becomes "Expired unpaid" and the listing goes back on offer if there is still time. If a payment is still processing at the deadline, we hold the food for up to 15 more minutes while the gateway confirms it.
- **First come, first served.** If two NGOs claim at the same moment, the platform gives the food to exactly one of them.
- **Late payments.** If a payment is confirmed after the hold ended and another NGO has the food, the late payment is refunded in full automatically.

An NGO can release a claim before paying with no penalty. Leaving 3 claims to expire unpaid within 7 days pauses claiming for 24 hours.

## 8. Payment and fees

- **How NGOs pay.** NGOs pay the full listing price when they claim, through our payment gateway PayFast: by Raast (the default) or a JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet. Card payments may be added later. Cash at pickup is never accepted.
- **What we never see.** You pay on PayFast's own page or in your bank's app. We never receive or store your card, bank or wallet login details.
- **Our fee.** Rizq Rasan keeps a commission of 5% of the listing price, services tax included, rounded down to the whole rupee. It comes out of the vendor's share. NGOs pay the listing price with nothing added. There is no sign-up fee and no monthly charge.
- **Gateway fees.** On normal sales we pay the gateway's fee out of our commission. When a payment is refunded, the gateway keeps its fee, so whoever caused the refund bears it.
- **Vendor payouts.** The vendor receives 95% of the listing price, less any tax the law requires us to withhold and the cost of any refund the vendor caused. Payouts go every Monday and Thursday, and we aim to pay within 6 working days of pickup.

For example: NGO pays Rs 1,200 · Vendor receives Rs 1,140 · Rizq Rasan Rs 60.

Receipts are always issued in the NGO's name. Our [Payments and Refunds]({{ '/legal/payments/' | relative_url }}) policy and the [Pricing]({{ '/pricing/' | relative_url }}) page give more detail.

## 9. Pickup codes and handover

- Once payment is confirmed, the NGO gets a 4-digit pickup code, the vendor's address and phone number, and a collector pass to send to its driver.
- The collector checks the food before the code is entered and may refuse food that looks unsafe.
- The vendor or its staff type in the code, confirm the quantity handed over and tap Confirm. Only this step marks the food as picked up.
- Five wrong codes within 10 minutes lock code entry for 10 minutes.
- The NGO can confirm receipt or report a problem within 24 hours of pickup. If it doesn't reply, the order is Completed. If neither side confirms anything, the order is Completed after 48 hours, the vendor is paid, and nobody's reliability changes.

Responsibility for the food passes from the vendor to the NGO at handover.
