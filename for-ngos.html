{% include section-head.html eyebrow="Who can join" title="Who can join" id="ngo-who-title" %}

Registered NGOs, shelters, community kitchens, food banks and orphanages in Islamabad can join, as long as the food is served or given to people for free.

We check your registration, who signs for you and where you work. Every organisation, and everyone who signs for one, is also screened against the UN Security Council sanctions list and Pakistan's proscribed-organisations and Fourth Schedule lists.

You'll promise never to resell food. Reselling claimed food, or uploading forged documents, means permanent removal.

We're signing NGOs first, in F-6, F-7 and Blue Area, and G-9 to G-11, F-10 and F-11, then the businesses within about 5 km of them.

{% include icon.html name="doc" size=22 %}

Registration we accept

  • ICT Charities Registration, Regulation and Facilitation Act 2021, preferred for organisations based in Islamabad
  • Societies Registration Act 1860
  • ICT Trust Act 2020
  • A not-for-profit company licensed by SECP under Section 42 of the Companies Act 2017
  • Voluntary Social Welfare Agencies Ordinance 1961

International NGOs register with the Ministry of Interior, not the Economic Affairs Division. If that's you, send us your Ministry of Interior registration.

{% include section-head.html eyebrow="Claim and pay" title="How to claim and pay" id="claim-title" lead="Paying when you claim is what holds the food for you. Raast is the default because the money arrives in seconds." %}

  1. Claim the food

    Find a listing near you and tap "Claim for Rs 1,200". Choose which collector will pick it up. The food is held for you and a countdown starts.

    The food is held at once

  2. Pay by Raast

    Approve the payment request in your own banking app, or scan the Raast QR. A JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet works too.

    Within 30 minutes, or by 15 minutes before pickup ends

  3. Get your pickup code

    As soon as the payment is confirmed, you see the 4-digit code, the vendor's address with a map link, and their phone number. Your receipt is ready to download.

    Seconds after paying

  4. Send your collector

    Send the collector pass to your driver by WhatsApp. They check the food, give the code at the counter and bring it back. Then confirm everything arrived, or report a problem, within 24 hours.

    During the pickup window
{% include icon.html name="phone" size=22 %}

Ways to pay

  • Raast, the default: approve a payment request in your banking app, or scan a QR.
  • JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet, the second choice.
  • Cards: planned for later.
  • Never: cash at pickup, or over-the-counter vouchers and shop payments, which take days to clear.

You pay on PayFast's own page or in your bank's app, so our servers never see your bank or wallet details.

Up to 3 people you name can pay for you. When you claim, each gets a pay link by email and WhatsApp, with no login needed. Receipts are always in your organisation's name.

Food safety after pickup {% include icon.html name="arrow-right" size=18 %}

The collector pass

  • Opens from a WhatsApp link, with no login.
  • Shows the pickup code, the vendor's address and the pickup time.
  • Works until 24 hours after the pickup window ends.
  • Lets the driver refuse food that looks unsafe, with a photo, for a full refund straight away.
  • Lets the driver report a problem, such as the vendor being closed.
  • Only the vendor typing in the code marks the food as picked up.
{% include section-head.html eyebrow="Pickup and no-show rules" title="Cancellations and no-shows" id="noshow-title" lead="Once you've paid, the vendor sets the food aside for you. These rules keep that fair on both sides." %}
{% include icon.html name="check" size=22 %}

Cancel early, full refund

Cancel at least 60 minutes before pickup starts and you get your money back in full. The food goes back to other organisations.

{% include icon.html name="x" size=22 %}

Cancel late or don't come

Later than that, or not collecting at all, counts as a no-show: no refund, the vendor is paid, and you get a strike. You can appeal within 24 hours.

{% include icon.html name="clock" size=22 %}

Not paid yet

Releasing food before you pay carries no penalty. Letting 3 claims expire unpaid in 7 days pauses claiming for 24 hours, but isn't a strike.

What happens at each number of strikes
Active strikes What happens
1A warning
2A 48-hour freeze on new claims
3A 7-day suspension and a review by our team

Strikes expire after 60 days, and each one comes with a written reason. We can forgive one strike every 90 days on appeal.

If you're running late, the vendor isn't asked whether you collected until 30 minutes after the pickup window ends.

Why isn't the food free?

A recovery price, capped at half of retail, pays the business back for its packing and handover time, so it keeps listing food instead of throwing it away.

The price you see is the price you pay. Rizq Rasan's 5% comes out of it, not on top, and covers the payment gateway's fees.

Organisations that have paid are also less likely to miss a pickup, which keeps vendors listing.

{% include icon.html name="receipt" size=22 %}

Why can't we pay cash at pickup?

Paying when you claim holds the food for you and protects the vendor from no-shows.

We couldn't confirm a cash payment, refund it if something went wrong, or give you a proper receipt. A Raast or wallet payment gets all three.
