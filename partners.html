Registered NGOs, shelters, community kitchens, food banks and orphanages. We sign NGOs first, then businesses within about 5 km of them.

Companies that want CSR reporting

Food chains can list surplus like any other food business, with the same licences and checks, and get a monthly impact certificate for their CSR report. Hotel groups and caterers will join once our safety processes are proven. If you'd like to support the work in another way, tell us how below.

{% include icon.html name="truck" size=20 %}

People who can help with pickups

Transport is one of the hardest parts for small NGOs. Volunteer drivers are planned after launch. If you could help collect food, tell us now and we'll get in touch when that starts.

  • {% include icon.html name="receipt" size=20 %}

    Businesses

    • Part of your cost back on food you couldn't sell
    • 80% of the listing price
    • No sign-up fee and no monthly charge
    • Payouts every Monday and Thursday
    • A monthly impact certificate for your CSR report
  • {% include icon.html name="users" size=20 %}

    NGOs and kitchens

    • Good food near you at no more than half of retail
    • Only businesses checked by a person
    • A receipt in your organisation's name for every payment
    • A full refund if the food isn't there or isn't safe
    • An impact summary you can share with donors
  • {% include icon.html name="sparkle" size=20 %}

    Pilot partners

    • A place in the January 2027 pilot, before listing and claiming open to everyone
    • No commission charged during the pilot
    • A say in how Rizq Rasan works: we test it with vendor staff and NGO coordinators on their own phones
    • Your name on this page, if you want it there
