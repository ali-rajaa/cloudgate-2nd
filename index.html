From surplus to sustenance.

Rizq Rasan will connect Islamabad's restaurants, bakeries and grocers that have good food left at the end of the day with verified NGOs that feed people. Vendors recover part of their cost, NGOs pay no more than half of retail, and the food gets eaten instead of thrown away.

{% include section-head.html title="Checks on both sides" id="trust-title" lead="Selling food, rather than giving it away, means food laws apply in full. So the rules are strict from day one." %}
  • {% include icon.html name="shield" size=20 %}

    Everyone is verified

    A person checks every business and NGO before it can list or claim: the owner's CNIC and proof the business is real, or the NGO's registration.

  • {% include icon.html name="thermometer" size=20 %}

    Food has time limits

    Each kind of food has a limit on how long it can be listed. No raw meat, poultry or fish, no home-cooked food, no plate leftovers.

  • {% include icon.html name="key" size=20 %}

    Pickup codes

    Food is handed over only when the NGO's driver gives the right 4-digit code at the counter.

  • {% include icon.html name="receipt" size=20 %}

    Money is held safely

    NGOs pay when they claim. The business is paid after pickup, and every refund case is written down in advance.
