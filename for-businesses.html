{% include section-head.html eyebrow="Who can join" title="Food businesses in Islamabad with the right licences" id="who-title" %}

Restaurants, bakeries, grocers and supermarkets, and cafes can list. Hotels and caterers can register their interest now and will join once our safety processes are proven. Every business needs an NTN and an Islamabad Food Authority licence. Restaurants and cafes also need their Department of Tourist Services registration.

We're starting close to the first NGOs we sign, in two zones: F-6, F-7 and Blue Area, and G-9 to G-11, F-10 and F-11. Bakeries come first, because their surplus is daily. Sign-ups open across Islamabad at launch.

At launch, each business lists from one pickup point. If you run several branches, tell us when you register.

{% include section-head.html eyebrow="Documents" title="What you'll need to send" id="documents-title" lead="Have these ready and verification takes 2 working days. If something is missing, we'll tell you exactly what, and only that part needs redoing." %}

Why an NTN? Since July 2025, FBR rules bar online marketplaces from serving sellers without one.

Once accounts open, you'll upload each document by camera or file, as a JPG, PNG or PDF of up to 10 MB. Only our verification team sees them. They're stored privately, every view is logged, and your signatory's CNIC is shown masked.

We'll also do a video call or a site visit with each of our first 20 partners.

Read the Verification Policy {% include icon.html name="arrow-right" size=18 %}

  • Business registration: your SECP certificate, partnership deed, or a sole proprietor's CNIC and declaration.
  • NTN: we check it against FBR's Active Taxpayer List.
  • Islamabad Food Authority licence.
  • Department of Tourist Services registration, for restaurants and cafes.
  • Signatory: the CNIC of the person who signs for the business, with a board resolution or authority letter.
  • Bank letter: an account maintenance certificate in the business name. Payouts go only to this account.
  • Address proof for your pickup point.
  • Optional, a halal certificate: your listings then show "halal-certified" instead of "vendor-declared".

{% include icon.html name="check" size=22 %}

What you can list from the start of the pilot

Tandoor breads, plain bakery, mithai, fruit and vegetables, packaged goods and drinks. Cooked food such as rice, curries and fried snacks is planned from 1 February 2027, if the pilot's first 4 weeks pass with no food incident.

{% include icon.html name="x" size=22 %}

Never allowed

Home-cooked food, plate returns or part-eaten food, food past its use-by date, opened dairy drinks, recalled products, alcohol and tobacco. No raw meat, poultry or fish at launch.

{% include icon.html name="thermometer" size=22 %}

A time limit for every category

Safe-until fills in from the category limit. You can shorten it but never extend it, and pickup must end at least 30 minutes before it. Cooked food can spend at most 2 hours at room temperature, and from May to September it must be hot-held or chilled.

{% include icon.html name="shield" size=22 %}

Yours until handover

Before publishing, you confirm the food was never served to customers, isn't a plate return, and has been stored correctly. You tell us about any food incident or Islamabad Food Authority action within 24 hours.
