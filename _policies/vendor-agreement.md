## 1. Parties and how this agreement is made

- **The parties.** The agreement is made with your business, not with the person who clicks to accept it. Our company details are on the [Legal Information]({{ '/legal/company/' | relative_url }}) page. {{ site.company.status }}.
- **Who accepts it.** A person aged 18 or over who is allowed to act for your business, shown by a board resolution or authority letter, accepts this agreement during onboarding. By accepting, they confirm they have that authority.
- **Accepting online.** Under the Electronic Transactions Ordinance 2002, accepting online has the same effect as signing on paper. We record the version you accepted, the language you saw it in, the time and the IP address.
- **When it starts.** This agreement takes effect when we approve your business. You can prepare draft listings while we review your application, but you cannot publish them until you are approved.
- **The pilot.** Partners invited before launch sign separate pilot terms, which apply until this agreement takes effect for them. During the pilot, NGOs pay vendors directly, no commission is charged and Rizq Rasan is not involved in the payment.

## 3. Your food safety promises

Rizq Rasan does not prepare, cook, own, store, pack, transport or inspect food. You are the seller, and the food is your responsibility until handover. For every listing, you promise that:

- the food is safe to eat, within its use-by date, not adulterated, not recalled, and yours to sell;
- it was never served to customers, and it is not a plate return or part-eaten food;
- it has been prepared, stored and handled lawfully and hygienically, with chilled food kept at 5°C or below and hot-held food at 60°C or above until handover;
- any cooked food stored chilled was chilled within 2 hours of cooking;
- the listing is true: the right category, the real quantity and a fair estimate of weight, and accurate times for when it was prepared, when it reached room temperature, and its label date;
- allergens and meat content (beef, mutton, chicken or fish) are marked correctly, with "may contain" ticked where your kitchen handles allergens that could cross into the food;
- your halal declaration is true. Halal shows as "vendor-declared" unless you have uploaded a halal certificate, and either way the declaration is yours;
- packaged goods are handed over within their label date, with their ingredients and date labels intact;
- you hold every licence and registration the law requires to sell the food.

Before each listing goes live, you tick two boxes: "This food was never served to customers and is not a plate return" and "This food has been stored correctly and is safe to eat." Ticking them confirms these promises for that listing. You are liable for any defect in the food at handover. The [Food Safety Policy]({{ '/legal/food-safety-policy/' | relative_url }}) forms part of this agreement.

## 4. Food you may list and its time limits

Categories open in stages, and the listing form shows only the categories that are open.

- **Open from the start:** tandoor breads, plain bakery, mithai and desserts without cream, fruit and vegetables, packaged and dry goods, and drinks.
- **Cooked food** (rice dishes, curries and similar dishes, fried snacks and iftar items) opens from 1 February 2027, and only if the pilot's first 4 weeks pass with no food incident.
- **Later, in Phase 2:** bakery with cream, custard or filling, and dairy.

Each category has a longest time it can be listed, depending on how it is stored:

| Category | Room temperature | Hot-held (60°C or more) | Chilled (5°C or less) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tandoor breads (naan, roti, paratha, sheermal) | 18 hours | Not allowed | 18 hours |
| Plain bakery (bread, buns, rusk, plain cake) | 24 hours | Not allowed | 24 hours |
| Bakery with cream, custard or filling | Not allowed | Not allowed | 6 hours |
| Cooked rice, curries, daal, haleem, nihari, salan, fried snacks and iftar items | 2 hours | 4 hours | 24 hours, if chilled within 2 hours of cooking |
| Mithai and desserts without cream | 48 hours | Not allowed | 48 hours |
| Fruit and vegetables | 72 hours | Not allowed | 72 hours |
| Dairy (milk, dahi, paneer, lassi) | Not allowed | Not allowed | Label date |
| Packaged and dry goods | Label date | Not allowed | Label date |
| Drinks and juices | Label date | Not allowed | Label date |

- **How the clock runs.** Cooked food, bakery and mithai count from "Prepared at". Cooked food kept at room temperature counts from "At room temperature since". Fruit and vegetables count from publishing. Packaged goods, dairy and drinks follow their label date. Frozen storage is allowed for packaged goods only, up to the label date. A mixed tray takes the strictest limit of the categories in it.
- **Safe-until.** The platform fills in the safe-until time from these limits. You can shorten it, but never extend it.
- **Hot months.** From May to September, cooked food cannot be listed at room temperature at all. It must be hot-held or chilled.
- **Never allowed:** food past its use-by date, home-cooked food, plate returns or part-eaten food, opened dairy drinks, recalled products, alcohol and tobacco. Raw meat, poultry and fish are not allowed at launch.

These limits will be confirmed with a food-safety adviser or the Islamabad Food Authority before launch. If they change, we will tell you before the change applies.

## 5. Listing rules

- **Sold whole.** A listing is one batch of food at one pickup point, at one price, sold whole to one NGO.
- **Price.** At least Rs 1,000 and no more than 50% of the food's retail value, in whole rupees. The retail value must be what the food normally sells for. Once the food is claimed, the price cannot change.
- **Photos.** 1 to 3 real photos of the actual food you are listing. The first photo is required. Stock images and photos of an earlier batch are not allowed.
- **Pickup window.** At least 30 minutes long, ending at least 60 minutes after you publish and at least 30 minutes before the safe-until time. Late-night windows are allowed.
- **Packaging.** Say whether the food is in sealed containers, in foil trays, or loose, in which case the NGO brings its own containers.
- **Real food only.** List only food you have, or will have ready, at the pickup point for the whole window.
- **Changes.** You can edit or withdraw a listing while it is Live, with no penalty. Once it is claimed, you can change only the wording, add photos, or update the pickup instructions.
- **Review.** Your first 3 listings, and any listing whose price, weight or photo looks unusual, are checked by our team before they go live. We may correct an obvious error in the category or times with a note, or remove a listing that breaks the rules. We always tell you the reason.

Live listings are shown only to approved NGOs, never on the public website.

## 6. Handover

- **Be ready.** Keep the food ready and correctly stored at your pickup point for the whole pickup window.
- **Let the collector check it.** The NGO's collector may check the food's look, smell and temperature before the code is entered, and may refuse food that looks unsafe.
- **Code first, then food.** Hand food over only when the collector gives the right 4-digit pickup code. You or your staff type the code, confirm the quantity actually handed over and tap Confirm. Only this step marks the food as picked up. Never confirm a handover that did not happen.
- **A different person.** The platform shows you the name of the collector the NGO expects. If someone else comes, you can call the NGO before handing over.
- **Wrong codes.** Five wrong codes within 10 minutes lock code entry for 10 minutes.
- **Late collectors.** A collector who arrives within 30 minutes after the window ends is late, not a no-show. After those 30 minutes, we ask you "Did they collect?". Answer truthfully. If they did not, the order becomes a No-show and you are paid as usual.

Responsibility for the food passes to the NGO at handover.

## 7. Rizq Rasan as your payment-collection agent

- **The appointment.** You appoint Rizq Rasan as your limited agent for one purpose: to collect the price of your orders from NGOs through our payment gateway, and to pay your share to you. We also refund NGOs for you in the cases this agreement sets out.
- **Paying us is paying you.** When an NGO pays Rizq Rasan for an order, the NGO has paid you, and its debt to you for that order is settled.
- **You remain the seller.** The sale is between you and the NGO. We never buy the food, never become its owner and never sell it.
- **How the money is held.** Money we collect for vendors sits in a collections account kept apart from company money until it is paid out. You hold no balance or wallet with us, and no interest is paid on money in the collections account.
- **What you allow us to do.** You allow us to register you with our payment gateway, PayFast, as a sub-merchant and to share the details it needs; to issue receipts to NGOs; to refund NGOs as this agreement sets out; and to deduct our commission, any tax we must withhold and the refund costs you caused before we pay you.
- **No other way to pay.** You must not ask an NGO to pay you in any other way, including cash at pickup, for food claimed through Rizq Rasan.

Before live payments start, this structure will be confirmed in writing with PayFast and in a written opinion from a Pakistani lawyer. If either requires a change, we will tell you as section 18 explains.

## 8. Our commission and what you receive

- **Commission.** Rizq Rasan keeps 5% of the listing price, services tax included, rounded down to the whole rupee in your favour.
- **Gateway fees.** On normal sales we pay the payment gateway's fee out of our commission.
- **Your share.** You receive 95% of the listing price, before any tax the law requires us to withhold. From that share we deduct any tax we must withhold and the costs of refunds you caused (section 10), and show each deduction line by line.
- **No other charges.** There is no sign-up fee, no monthly charge and no listing fee. NGOs pay the listing price with nothing added.

| Listing price (NGO pays) | Rizq Rasan's commission | You receive, before tax withheld by law |

When an order is refunded in full, no commission is charged. When it is partly refunded, the commission falls in proportion. On a No-show, the commission is charged as usual. ## 9. Payouts and holds

- **When an order becomes payable.** When it is Completed, or a confirmed No-show, and our payment gateway has settled the money into the collections account, usually 2 to 3 working days after payment. An order is Completed when the NGO confirms receipt, or when 24 hours pass after pickup with no problem reported. If neither side confirms anything, it is Completed (unconfirmed) after 48 hours and you are paid.
- **Payout days.** We pay every Monday and Thursday, by bank transfer or Raast bulk payment, only to the verified bank account in your business name. We aim to pay within 6 working days of pickup.
- **Two people approve every payout.** One of our admins prepares each payout batch and a second approves it, so no single person can move money alone.
- **What you see.** Each order shows its status in your earnings: Pending, Eligible, In batch, Paid or On hold. Each payout comes with an email listing every order, our commission, any tax withheld and the bank reference, with a tax invoice for our commission.

We hold back money only in these cases:

| When | What is held | Until |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An order is disputed | That order only. Your other orders are paid as normal | The dispute is decided |
| You change your payout account | All your payouts, and the owner gets an email, to stop fraud | 48 hours have passed |
| Card payments are on (Phase 2) | 5% of your card sales, as a reserve | 30 days have passed |

## 10. Refunds you cause, and clawbacks

Payment gateways keep their fee when a payment is refunded, so the rule is simple: whoever caused the refund bears that fee, and any tax already withheld on the refunded amount.

| What happens | NGO gets back | Our commission | Who bears the gateway fee and any tax already withheld |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You cancel after the NGO paid | 100% | Not charged | You |
| Food refused at pickup, before the code is entered | 100%, straight away | Not charged | You, if we uphold the refusal. Otherwise Rizq Rasan |
| Problem reported after pickup and upheld | Full or partial, as decided | Falls in proportion | You |
| You hand over less than listed | The missing share, automatically | Falls in proportion | You |
| You were closed or the food was not ready | 100% | Not charged | You |
| NGO cancels at least 60 minutes before pickup starts | 100% | Not charged | Rizq Rasan |
| NGO cancels later, or does not turn up | Nothing | Charged | Nobody extra. You are paid |
| Late, duplicate or failed payment | 100%, automatically | Not charged | Rizq Rasan |

- **How we recover it.** Refund costs you caused are deducted from your next payout and shown line by line. Every refund reverses our commission in the ledger and, where tax was charged on it, we issue a credit note.
- **If there is nothing to deduct from.** If an amount you owe cannot be deducted from a payout, for example because you have closed your account, it remains a debt you owe us and we will invoice you for it.

The [Payments and Refunds]({{ '/legal/payments/' | relative_url }}) policy sets out every case and how long each refund takes.

## 11. Cancellations, closures and short handovers

- **Cancelling after payment.** Once an NGO has paid, it has set aside time and transport for your food. Cancel only if you truly cannot hand it over, and do it as early as you can. The NGO is refunded in full, no commission is charged, you bear the refund costs, and you get a strike. We offer the NGO up to 3 similar listings.
- **Closed or not ready.** If the collector arrives within the pickup window and you are closed or the food is not ready, the collector reports it from the collector pass. If you do not reply within 2 hours, the NGO is refunded in full and you get a strike. If you do reply, we decide the case as a dispute.
- **Short handover.** If you hand over less than you listed, enter the real quantity at handover. The NGO is refunded for the missing share automatically, rounded to the rupee, and our commission falls in proportion. Handing over more than 10% short gives you a strike.

For example, you list 40 naan for Rs 2,000 and hand over 30. The NGO gets Rs 500 back automatically and our commission falls to Rs 75. You receive Rs 1,425, less the refund costs on the Rs 500 refunded, and you get a strike because the handover was 25% short.

## 12. Staff accounts

- **Pickup-only role.** The owner can invite counter or shift staff, who get a pickup-only role. Staff can see the day's pickups and enter pickup codes. They cannot list food, change your details or see money.
- **Own logins.** Everyone who acts for your business uses their own login. Shared logins are not allowed, because they make it impossible to tell who did what.
- **Your responsibility.** You are responsible for everything done through your staff accounts, as if you had done it. Remove a person's access as soon as they leave or no longer need it.
- **Training.** Make sure your staff know the handover rules in section 6: let the collector check the food, hand over only on the right code, and confirm the real quantity.
- **Payout account.** Only the owner can change the payout account. Any change pauses payouts for 48 hours.

## 13. Reliability, strikes and appeals

Every vendor has a reliability score from 0 to 100, built from its orders in the last 90 days. A vendor whose score falls below 60 may have at most 3 live listings until our team reviews it.

You get a strike for:

- cancelling an order after the NGO has paid;
- being closed, or not having the food ready, when the collector comes within the window;
- handing over more than 10% less than you listed;
- an upheld food-safety complaint, including food refused at pickup where we uphold the refusal.

| Active strikes | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A warning |
| 2 | A 48-hour freeze on new listings |
| 3 | A 7-day suspension and a review by our team |

Strikes expire after 60 days. Every strike comes with a written reason. You can appeal a strike within 24 hours and a suspension within 7 days, and a person reviews every appeal. We can forgive one strike per 90 days on appeal. No strike is given for a missed deadline or pickup caused by an internet, power or payment network outage. Uploading forged or altered documents means permanent removal. The [Reliability and No-show Policy]({{ '/legal/reliability/' | relative_url }}) has the full rules.

## 14. Food incidents, insurance and indemnity

- **Tell us within 24 hours** about any food incident linked to food you listed, such as someone reporting illness, and about any action by the Islamabad Food Authority against your business, such as a fine, sealing, or suspension of your licence.
- **What we do in an incident.** We can freeze all your live listings in the same category at once and tell every NGO that collected food from the same batch. We keep the photos and the order timeline, and we cooperate with the Islamabad Food Authority, which may include sharing the order's details with it.
- **Your cooperation.** You agree to answer our questions promptly, share your records for the batch, such as preparation times and storage, and follow any direction from the authorities.
- **Insurance.** We encourage you to carry product liability insurance for the food you sell. It is not a condition of joining at launch.
- **Indemnity.** To the extent the law allows, you agree to cover Rizq Rasan's reasonable losses and costs, and claims made against us, that arise from unsafe, wrongly described or unlawfully sold food you listed, from your breach of this agreement or the law, or from the acts of your staff. This does not apply to loss caused by our own negligence or fraud.
- **Our liability to you** is limited as section 15 of the [Terms of Service]({{ '/legal/terms/' | relative_url }}) explains.

## 15. Records, statements and tax

- **Statements.** You can see every order and download statements from your earnings. Each payout comes with a remittance email and a tax invoice for our commission, and we issue a credit note when we reverse commission on which tax was charged.
- **Your own taxes.** You are responsible for your own taxes on the food you sell, and for your own accounts. We do not give tax advice.
- **Tax we withhold.** If the law requires us, or our payment gateway, to withhold tax on your sales, for example under section 6A of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001, we deduct it, show it line by line and give you the evidence of the deduction that the law provides for. Whether these rules apply to food sold through Rizq Rasan is being confirmed with a tax adviser before launch.
- **Reporting to FBR.** Since 1 July 2025, FBR rules bar online marketplaces from serving sellers without an NTN and require monthly statements on sellers. If these rules apply to us, we will include your details in those statements. Keep your NTN active, and tell us if it or your Active Taxpayer List status changes.
- **What we keep.** We keep your documents and payment records for the life of your account plus 6 years, as section 174 of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001 requires. Our [Data Retention]({{ '/legal/data-retention/' | relative_url }}) policy explains the rest.
- **What you keep.** Keep your own records of the food you listed and handed over, including preparation times and storage, for as long as the law requires, and show them to us or to the Islamabad Food Authority in a food-safety case.

## 17. Term, suspension and termination

- **Length.** This agreement lasts from your approval until it is ended by you or by us.
- **Ending it yourself.** You can close your account at any time once you have no open orders. Money already payable to you is paid in the normal payout runs, less any amounts you owe.
- **Suspension or removal by us.** We can suspend or remove your business if you break this agreement, the terms or the law, put food safety or other users at risk, or no longer meet the conditions in section 2. Uploading forged documents means permanent removal. We always give a written reason, and you can appeal a suspension within 7 days.
- **What suspension means.** Your listings are hidden and your paid orders are refunded to the NGOs. You keep read access to your records.
- **What survives.** Money owed either way, refunds and clawbacks, your indemnity, limits of liability and record-keeping continue after this agreement ends.
